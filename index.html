Questions travellers ask
before they book

How do I book?

Send one WhatsApp message with your date, time, pick-up, destination and — if you're flying — your flight number. Junior replies with a fixed total in US dollars. When you say yes, it's booked. There is no app to download, no account, and no deposit for standard transfers.

Do you accept cash?

Yes — cash is the easiest way to pay, handed to your driver at the end of the ride. US dollars and Dominican pesos are both fine. Pesos are converted at the day's rate. If you'd rather not carry cash, ask about card or bank transfer when you book and Junior will arrange it in advance.

Do you offer round trips?

Yes, and booking both directions at once is the smart move — your return is locked into the schedule and Junior confirms the pick-up time for your departure day. Each leg is charged at the normal fixed rate; nothing is added for the return. If your flight home moves, message him and the pick-up moves with it.

Is the price really fixed?

Yes. The number you agree on WhatsApp is the number you pay. There is no meter, no night surcharge, no luggage fee and no per-person charge — one price covers the vehicle for up to six passengers. The only thing that changes a price is you changing the trip: adding a stop, a second destination, or waiting time.

Can I pay in USD?

All prices on this page are quoted in US dollars and US dollars are accepted everywhere Junior drives. Dominican pesos, Canadian dollars and euros can also be arranged — just mention which currency you're carrying when you book, so the exact amount is agreed before you travel.

How do I find my driver at the airport?

Clear immigration and baggage, walk out of the arrivals doors at Puerto Plata (POP), and look for your name on a sign — Junior is waiting in the meeting area just outside, not in the car park. He tracks your flight, so a delay doesn't matter. If you can't spot him, send a WhatsApp message the moment you land and he'll come to you.

How far in advance should I book?

For airport transfers, 24 hours is comfortable and a week is ideal in high season (December–April, and July–August). For a taxi around Sosúa or Cabarete, an hour's notice is usually plenty — and often much less. Junior operates 24/7, so late-night and pre-dawn departures are normal, not an exception.

Do you have child seats?

Yes, at no extra charge — just tell Junior your children's ages when you book so the right seat is fitted before he leaves for the airport. Families travelling with a pushchair and several suitcases should mention it too, so a minivan is sent instead of a car.
