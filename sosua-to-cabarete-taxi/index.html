Sosúa to Cabarete: US$15 fixed, round trip whenever you want

By the Junior Martinez Transfer team · Updated September 2026

Cabarete is one hop from Sosúa: kitesurfing, beachfront restaurants and the bachata classes that made the North Coast famous. Junior gets you there and back for a price that doesn't change at midnight.

Price and duration

A fixed US$15 per vehicle (up to 6 passengers), with a trip of about 15 minutes between Sosúa and central Cabarete. The same price applies for the return trip.

Why it's worth booking the return too

Cabarete's nightlife runs late, and transport gets scarce in the small hours. The practical move is to give Junior your approximate return time when you book the outbound leg, so your car is guaranteed no matter how late it gets.

What to do in Cabarete once you arrive

Cabarete beach, famous for kitesurfing and windsurfing; restaurants right on the sand; and, for something different, a salsa or bachata class with Salsa Bachata Cabarete. You can read more in our guide to Sosúa, which includes a full section on Cabarete.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a taxi from Sosúa to Cabarete cost?

A fixed US$15 per vehicle, for up to 6 passengers, on a trip of about 15 minutes.

Can you pick me up late at night in Cabarete?

Yes, the service runs 24 hours. It's best to give Junior your approximate return time when you book the outbound trip.

How long does the trip take?

About 15 minutes without traffic. Junior confirms the estimated time when you book.
