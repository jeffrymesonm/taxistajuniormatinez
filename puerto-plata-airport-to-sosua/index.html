Puerto Plata Airport (POP) to Sosúa: US$30, no surprises

By the Junior Martinez Transfer team · Updated September 2026

Gregorio Luperón International Airport (POP) is 15 minutes from Sosúa. Junior meets you at arrivals with a sign with your name and a price you already know before you land.

Price and travel time

A fixed US$30 per vehicle (1 to 6 passengers, not per person), with a trip of about 15 minutes between the airport and Sosúa. If you book a round trip, each leg is the same price — no extra charge for waiting on your return flight.

How your driver finds you

After clearing immigration and collecting your luggage, walk out through the arrivals doors and look for the sign with your name. Junior tracks your flight, so he knows if it's early or delayed. If you don't see him right away, message him on WhatsApp as soon as you land.

What's included

An air-conditioned vehicle, help with your luggage, and a direct transfer to your hotel or villa in Sosúa. Cash is accepted in US dollars or Dominican pesos, with no night surcharge and no hidden fees.

Frequently asked questions

How much does the POP airport to Sosúa transfer cost?

A fixed US$30 per vehicle, for up to 6 passengers. The price doesn't change based on traffic or arrival time.

What if my flight is delayed?

Junior tracks your flight number, so the pickup time adjusts automatically. There's no extra charge for a delay.

Do you also do the Sosúa to airport trip (round trip)?

Yes. You can book the arrival and departure transfers together, each leg at the same fixed US$30 price.
