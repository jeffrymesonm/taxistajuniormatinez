How Pickup Works

  1. 1Message Junior before you sailwith your ship's name, cruise port (Amber Cove or Taino Bay) and arrival date. He'll confirm the exact meeting point for that port.
  2. 2Your driver waits for youat the agreed meeting point near the terminal exit once your ship has cleared immigration, with a sign showing your name.
  3. 3You agree a "back by" time togetherbefore heading out, built around your ship's all-aboard deadline with a safety margin.
  4. 4Pay in cash— US dollars or Dominican pesos — once you're back, or as agreed when you confirm the booking.

Plan the return first. Decide your "must be back at the ship" time before you plan the day, then build the itinerary backward from it with a comfortable buffer. Nobody can guarantee traffic or road conditions, and a private pickup doesn't carry the cruise line's own return-to-ship protection.

Amber Cove or Taino Bay?

They're two separate terminals a short drive apart. Tell Junior which one your ship uses when you message him — he'll confirm the meeting point for that specific port.

Amber Cove

On Maimón Bay, west of downtown Puerto Plata. Used mainly by Carnival Corporation brands.

Taino Bay

Right next to the historic center of Puerto Plata. Closer to the Malecón and Umbrella Street.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you really pick up right at the cruise port?

Yes — pickup is confirmed at both Amber Cove and Taino Bay. Message Junior with your ship and arrival date before you sail and he'll confirm the exact meeting point for that port.

How will I find my driver?

Junior will wait at the agreed meeting point near the terminal exit with a sign showing your name, once your details are confirmed on WhatsApp.

What if the ship arrives late?

Message Junior on WhatsApp as soon as you know, and he'll adjust the pickup time. Keep your phone reachable in case plans shift on the day.

How much does a transfer from the cruise port cost?

It depends on your destination, group size and how long you'll need the vehicle. Send your port, date and plans on WhatsApp for a price before you book — nothing is charged until you confirm.

Is this a private vehicle or a shared shuttle?

Private. The vehicle is for your group only, with no other passengers and no fixed schedule to wait on.

What if I'm not back in time for all-aboard?

A private pickup booked outside the cruise line doesn't carry the ship's own return-to-ship protection. Agree a "must be back" time with Junior before you head out, and build in a generous buffer.
