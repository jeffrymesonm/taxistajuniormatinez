Is there Uber in Puerto Plata and Sosúa? What to use instead

By the Junior Martinez Transfer team · Published November 2026

Short answer: the Uber app lists Puerto Plata, but on the Sosúa–Cabarete coast it is not something you should plan a flight, a dinner or a day trip around. Here is what to expect, and what travellers actually use instead.

Where Uber works, and where it doesn't

Expat guides list Puerto Plata among the Dominican cities with Uber, alongside Santo Domingo and Santiago (ExpatFocus, reviewed May 2026). That does not mean a car will appear every time you open the app in Sosúa or Cabarete.

  • Airport pickups: ride-comparison sites such as RideGuru list rideshares as unavailable at Puerto Plata Airport (POP).
  • Evenings and late nights: fewer drivers online means longer waits or no car at all.
  • Between towns: a driver has to accept a longer trip in advance, and cancellations are common.
We are a local driver service, not a review site: check the app yourself on the day. Availability changes, and the point is not to depend on it when timing matters.

How taxis work here

There are no meters on the North Coast. Fares are agreed before the ride, which is why the same trip can be quoted at very different prices. Two questions fix most problems: is the price per person or per vehicle? and does it change at night or with luggage?

What travellers use instead

  • A pre-booked private driver for the airport, late nights and day trips: you know the price and the driver knows when you land.
  • Guaguas (local minibuses) on the main road: very cheap, crowded, no door-to-door and little space for luggage.
  • Motoconchos (motorcycle taxis) for short hops in town, one passenger and no bags.

When it's worth booking ahead

  • Arriving at Puerto Plata Airport (POP), where a delayed landing should not cost you a ride.
  • Leaving Cabarete after dinner or a bachata night.
  • Groups of three or more with luggage.
  • Day trips and cruise-port transfers.

Frequently asked questions

Is there Uber in Sosúa?

Uber is listed for Puerto Plata, but coverage on the Sosúa and Cabarete stretch of the North Coast is inconsistent, especially at night and for airport pickups. If you have a flight or a fixed time, pre-book a driver instead of relying on the app.

Does Uber pick up at Puerto Plata Airport (POP)?

Ride-comparison sites list rideshares as unavailable at Puerto Plata Airport. Plan on a pre-booked transfer or an airport taxi; Junior's POP to Sosúa transfer is a fixed US$30 per vehicle for up to 6 passengers.
