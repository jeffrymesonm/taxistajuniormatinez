Taxi prices in Sosúa, Cabarete and Puerto Plata: 2026 fixed-rate guide

By the Junior Martinez Transfer team · Published November 2026

Dominican taxis have no meters, so the price you pay depends on what you agree before getting in. These are the fixed prices Junior charges from Sosúa, per vehicle, so you can budget before you land.

Prices are in US dollars, per vehicle for 1–6 passengers, the same day or night and in either direction. Drive times are estimates and depend on traffic. Cash in US$ or DOP is fine. Junior confirms the exact price on WhatsApp before you travel.

How the pricing works

  • One price per vehicle, not per person: a family of five pays the same as a couple.
  • No night surcharge. The service runs 24 hours and the price stays the same.
  • Flight tracked. For airport pickups Junior follows your flight, so a late landing does not change the price.
  • Child seats are available on request at no charge.

Questions to ask any taxi driver

  1. Is the price per person or per vehicle?
  2. Does it change at night or with extra luggage?
  3. Is it the final price, with no stops or waiting charges added?
  4. Can I have it confirmed in writing, for example on WhatsApp?

Frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Puerto Plata Airport to Sosúa?

A fixed US$30 per vehicle for up to 6 passengers, about 15 minutes.

How much is a taxi from Sosúa to Cabarete?

A fixed US$15 per vehicle, about 15 minutes, with the same price for the return trip.

Is the price per person or per vehicle?

Per vehicle, for 1 to 6 passengers, day or night. You do not pay extra per person.

What about trips that don't start in Sosúa?

The rate board is measured from Sosúa. Trips between other towns are quoted individually on WhatsApp, so send your pick-up and drop-off points.
